## Fixes

- The view-transition preset of the client runtime goes in the `bongo` cascade layer, so a rule of your own overrides it without `!important`. Before, the runtime appended the preset to `<head>` after the stylesheets of the page, and source order gave it the last word: a page could turn off the cross-fade of `transitions` only with `!important`, or by giving up the preset for `'initial'`. A rule that already reaches for `!important` still wins, because the preset carries none of its own. See [Turn on transitions](/view-transitions/#turn-on-transitions) and [Cascade layers](/css/#cascade-layers). ([fced932](https://github.com/lukeed/bongo/commit/fced932))
